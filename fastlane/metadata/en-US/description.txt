“Herr Ober!” turns a public-domain textbook from 1899 into a reading and listening experience. “Servierkunde” (The Art of Serving) was published in Vienna for apprentice waiters: how to greet guests, how to lay a banquet table for 32, which glass goes with which wine.

The book was digitised page by page, the German text carefully re-typed and recorded with a narrator voice. You read every page either in Fraktur blackletter with the long ſ or in a modern, easy-to-read typeface – and switch with one tap.

READ ALOUD WITH HIGHLIGHTING
Every chapter is read aloud. The spoken word is highlighted in yellow while the text scrolls along. Speed 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.2. Works in both scripts.

WORD LENS
Tap a word in Fraktur: it appears large in modern script and is spoken.

FRAKTUR PRIMER
The blackletter alphabet, the tricky letters (ſ and s, k and f, A and U) and a small recognition exercise with words from the book.

54 PLATES AND 14 MENUS
China, glassware, cutlery, place settings and banquet tables as gently restored engravings – with the original scan for comparison, zoom and captions in German and French. Plus historical menus, including a royal luncheon from 1894.

READING COMFORT
Adjustable text size and line spacing, light and dark mode, VoiceOver, reading progress kept on your device.

The reading texts use modern German spelling in both typefaces. Image captions and menu cards retain their historical spelling. The app interface is available in English and German.

A project of DVLD e. V. (Dyslexia Research Center) together with the EÖDL. Free, no ads, no data collection, fully offline.
